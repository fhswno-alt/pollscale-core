# Consumer onboarding

Runs after Apple or Google sign-in. The three guest votes stay optional and generic.

- Guest votes, then sign in → onboarding next
- Sign in immediately → onboarding right then

Collect first name, username (same reserved / blocklist rules), date of birth (13+), optional city, then interests.

Interests are parent categories with subcategories (Technology → AI, Robotics). At least three parent topics. The same picker is in **You → Interests** later.
